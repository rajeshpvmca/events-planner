COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to our most common inquiries regarding our bespoke planning, international destinations, and personalized coordination.

How far in advance should we book your event planning services?

We recommend reserving dates 6 to 12 months in advance for luxury weddings and grand galas, though we gladly accommodate corporate timelines within 2 to 3 months.

Do you handle destination events and international celebrations?

Yes! Our team produces exquisite destination weddings and private celebrations across Europe, the Americas, and the Caribbean with full travel logistics.

Can you work with our pre-selected vendors or venue?

Absolutely. We seamlessly collaborate with your preferred venues or vendors while bringing our rigorous coordination standards to the partnership.

What distinguishes full-service planning from month-of coordination?

Full-service covers comprehensive concept ideation, styling, vendor management, and contract review from day one, while coordination focuses on executing your existing plans in the final weeks.
