Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our bespoke planning process, venues, vendors, and services.

How far in advance should we book our event?

We recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance for weddings and major galas, and 2 to 4 months for intimate celebrations and corporate gatherings.

Can we bring in our own preferred vendors?

Absolutely. While we maintain a curated list of trusted premium vendors, we are delighted to collaborate with any licensed vendors you select.

Do you handle destination events or outdoor venues?

Yes! We specialize in vineyard, private estate, and coastal destination celebrations with complete logistics and weather contingency planning.

What is included in day-of event coordination?

Day-of coordination covers comprehensive schedule oversight, vendor arrivals, cue management, guest navigation, and complete takedown oversight.
